How to Split a Bill Fairly

Splitting bills among friends doesn't have to be complicated. Our free bill splitter calculator helps you divide restaurant checks, travel expenses, rent, and any shared costs quickly and accurately. Simply add participants, enter what each person ordered, specify who paid, and get instant results showing exactly who owes whom.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the bill splitting calculator work?

Our bill splitter uses a smart algorithm to minimize the number of transactions needed. First, add all people sharing the bill. Then, add each item with its price and who paid for it. The calculator automatically determines the optimal way to settle debts, so instead of everyone paying everyone, you get the minimum number of transfers needed.

Can I split bills with different items per person?

Yes! Unlike simple bill dividers that just split the total equally, our calculator lets you assign specific items to specific people. This is perfect for restaurant bills where everyone ordered different dishes at different prices. Each person only pays for what they actually consumed.

What currencies are supported?

Bill Splitter automatically detects your language and sets the appropriate currency. We support USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), RUB (₽), UAH (₴), and PLN (zł). You can manually switch between 6 languages: English, German, Spanish, Polish, Russian, and Ukrainian.

Is my data saved anywhere?

Your privacy is important. All calculations happen locally in your browser. Bill history is saved only on your device using local storage. We don't collect, store, or transmit any of your financial data to external servers. You can clear your history anytime from your browser settings.

Can I use this for recurring shared expenses?

Absolutely! Bill Splitter is great for roommates splitting rent and utilities, travel groups sharing accommodation costs, office teams dividing lunch orders, or any situation where multiple people share expenses. Save your bills to history and load them later as templates.

Does it work offline?

Yes! Once the page loads, Bill Splitter works completely offline. This makes it perfect for splitting bills at restaurants with poor internet connection. Your saved bill history is also available offline since it's stored locally on your device.
